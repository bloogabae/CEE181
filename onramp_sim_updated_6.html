IDM driver parameters – edit, then press Apply & reset

Parameter Symbol Unit Mean μ Std dev σ Min Max

Each car draws aggressiveness agg, v₀, s₀, and b from a bounded normal distribution (mean μ, std dev σ, truncated to [min, max]). Aggressiveness maps T = T_max − agg·(T_max − T_min) and a = a_min + agg·(a_max − a_min) so more aggressive drivers accept shorter headways and accelerate faster. Set σ = 0 for identical drivers. Edits take effect on Reset.
